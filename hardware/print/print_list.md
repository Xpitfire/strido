# Print list

Generated by `strido_publish.print_files`. Every part (upstream Yertle and Strido) prints from `hardware/print/stl/`, oriented for printing; `hardware/print/plates/` holds ready 3MF plates.

Servo mounts and `servo_mount_top_bracket`: print with X-Y hole compensation -0.15 mm so their M3 pilots grip (ADR-009); otherwise harden those pilots with a drop of thin CA glue before driving the screws (anaerobic thread locker is not for plastic: it does not cure there and can crack PETG). The compensation acts on vertical holes: the servo mounts lie like a U, so their hip-horn and hip-pivot pilots are horizontal and get the CA treatment if a screw spins.

Hole steps (a bearing seat narrowing to a smaller hole above it) are cut for bridging in the STLs: the two layers above the step print a slot, then a square (see the notes). Where the note says so, block supports in the bearing bore in the slicer: the seat sits above the bed and would otherwise be supported.

Settings (all parts unless noted): PETG or PLA+, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls (1.2 mm), 5 top/bottom layers, 30 % gyroid infill, snug supports where listed, brim on parts taller than 40 mm. PrusaSlicer profile: `hardware/print/prusaslicer.ini` (File > Import > Import Config). Checked: closed manifold, fits a 220 x 220 x 250 mm bed, and every part and plate slices without stability warnings with exactly these settings (`make printability`, docs/verification/printability.md).
