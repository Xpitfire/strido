## Unreleased

### Added
- German documentation site under `/de/` (language switch in the top bar): every page, the home page, navigation, search, shop, print kit, catalogue and 3D-viewer texts. Pages are rendered at build time from translation catalogues (`i18n/de/`, one per page and data file; `docs-site/scripts/i18n.mjs`, `i18n-catalogues.mjs`), so generated pages keep their translation when regenerated and changed numbers carry over; German headings keep the English anchors. `make i18n` extracts new segments; `make drift` fails on an untranslated segment or a translation that breaks the Markdown. Style and terminology (established English technical terms stay English): `i18n/README.md`.
- AI assistant on every page (`docs-site/content/.vitepress/theme/ai/`, service `assistant/`): a small bar that `/` expands into a floating panel in the middle of the screen, pinnable as a right sidebar; conversations with history (new, switch, rename, delete), answers streamed with sources from the documentation (English or German), images and text attached by paste, drag and drop or upload. The service searches the pages (SQLite FTS5) and calls the model through Cortex Keyring.
- Agent access to the knowledge base (`docs/ai.md`): MCP server `/mcp` (`search_docs`, `read_page`, `list_pages`, `search_parts`) for agents with a personal Cortex app token, the Agent Skill `skills/strido/SKILL.md` (also as `/downloads/strido-skill.zip` with every page in both languages), `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` (English and German).
- Printability gate `strido_verify.printability` (`make printability`, part of `make check`): every printed part and every 3MF plate is sliced by PrusaSlicer with the kit profile `hardware/print/prusaslicer.ini`; slicer errors, tool-path conflicts, missing parts and print-stability issues fail (for supported parts the stability analysis runs on an extra unsupported slice and keeps the issues supports cannot fix); wall-thickness scan for walls, fins and skins thinner than one or two lines/layers; filament and time per part.
- Electronics voltage domains: every supply input and logic signal checked against its rated range (`rails`, `supplies`, `signals` in `hardware/electronics.yaml`); over-voltage fails, a rail minimum below a rating is reported without margin.
- Documentation site on Cortex at https://strido.cortex.a2olabs.com/ behind SSO (`.cortex/app.yml`, `docs-site/Dockerfile`), with a Downloads page: build kit ZIP (all STLs, 3MF plates, slicer profile, print list, book PDF, BOM), the book PDF and the 3D model.
- Docs site layout: wide tables scroll inside the text column (never under the page outline), shop URLs show as their domain (full address on hover), and the "On this page" outline can be hidden for full-width reading; the print list shows file paths relative to `hardware/print/`.
- Shopping list rewritten as four alternative complete plans with a choice guide, totals vs. the cheapest and a per-item comparison; the research page is labelled as notes.
- Parts shop (`docs/shop.md`; data `docs/bom/shop.json` from `strido_publish.shopping`, logic `docs-site/.../theme/shop/cart.ts`): the complete robot as a kit product with plan choice, every part as a product card with render, shop offers and stock, a cart drawer with quantities, per-shop shipping and total, and a checkout per shop (one-click Amazon cart, copyable order lists with item links, CSV).
- Print kit builder (`docs/print-kit.md`): every printed part with render, size, filament and time; choose complete robots or single parts and download a ready-to-print ZIP built in the browser (STLs named with their quantity, slicer profile, parts sheet, and the 3MF plates for complete robots).
- Product pages in the shop and print kit (click a part; shareable `#part=<id>` links): description, features, specifications, all shop offers, where the part sits (assembly step links, 3D viewer) and Learn links. Content in `hardware/products.yaml`, exported by `make bom` to `docs/bom/products.json`; `make bom` and a test fail when a BOM line or printed part has no entry or links a missing wiki page.
- Learn wiki (`docs/wiki/`, own sidebar): 18 articles on servos, LiPo batteries, power, microcontrollers, buses, sensors, mechanics, Linux on the Pi, Docker, ROS 2, firmware, kinematics and gaits, simulation and RL, CAD as code, 3D printing, fasteners, tools and safety, plus a glossary; each grounded in this robot's parts and code, with self-check questions and primary sources.
- Site: cart indicator in the top bar, assembly progress checkboxes per step (stored in the browser), stable `#step-N` anchors for assembly steps, "What it takes" summary on the home page, verification logs served by the site, catalogue STL download and step links, 3D viewer highlights the picked part and links to the print kit.
- Strength gate `strido_verify.strength` (part of `make verify`): femur and tibia bending in the leg plane at the weakest printed section (walls only), link tension and Euler buckling, screw shear, hole bearing pressure, bearing static loads, and a tightening-torque limit for every printed pilot (pull-out τ · π · d · L_e, T = k · F · d); loads from the servo stall torque and a 3 × weight landing (`hardware/strength.yaml`, PETG/screw/bearing data cited). 19 checks; the knee linkage is swept over every pose inside the joint limits. Lowest S = 0.34: the upstream long link buckles in the leg plane at the knee servo's stall force (holds standing, S ≈ 2.2); recorded as a known weak point in ADR-010 (bounded acceptance `min_safety`, builder action) until a stiffer Strido long link replaces it. Results appear on each part's page; Learn article "Strength, torque and failure".
- Guided checkout per shop: a checklist opens each item's shop page in one reused tab with the quantity and order code to enter; progress is stored in the browser.
- Equations on the site are typeset with LaTeX (VitePress math, `markdown-it-mathjax3` 4.3.2, rendered to SVG at build time); every wiki formula and the strength report use it. Wide equations scroll on phones.
- Learn wiki "Robotics theory" section, seven articles worked on Strido's own leg, servos and sensors: frames and rotations (rotation matrices, quaternions, homogeneous transforms, the tf tree), kinematics (forward/inverse kinematics of the leg, knee four-bar, Jacobian, singularities), statics and dynamics (virtual work, τ = Jᵀ F, mechanical advantage, inertia, torque and power), control (PD/PID, second-order response, loop rates, the servo's internal loop), legged locomotion (gaits, support polygon, ZMP, capture point, Raibert foot placement, the trot generator), state estimation (IMU models, complementary/Mahony/Kalman filters, odometry) and reinforcement learning (MDP, policy gradient, GAE, PPO, the Strido environment, sim-to-real). The existing pages gained the underlying theory (Ohm's law and wire losses, buck converters, PWM, I²C rise time, bearing life, LiPo energy).
- Wiring harness in the CAD (ADR-011): `hardware/wiring.yaml` names every cable (W00–W23 and the 12 servo leads), the connector each end sits on and the tie points it passes; `strido_cad.wiring` routes the legs between them through the robot's free space (every base part and every volume a leg sweeps over its joint limits kept out) and adds conductors, hip loops, spiral-wrap sleeves, cable ties and two adhesive tie mounts to the assembly, so the renders (new `wiring.png`), the 3D viewer, the assembly manual and the model masses include them (the lumped 0.12 kg harness estimate is gone).
- Wiring gate `strido_verify.wiring` (part of `make verify`, report `docs/verification/wiring.md` with the cut list): ready-made leads reach with 10 mm to spare, wire/spiral-wrap/tie stock covers the cut list, each lead across a hip joint has a loop ≥ 1.2 × the farthest its ties get apart over the hip range, one cable per WAGO entry and PCA9685 channel, no conductor through a part and ≥ 1 mm to every part of another body in all 13 operating poses; cut branches count with their slack and crimp. 174 checks, 0 fail, 1 warn (LiDAR cable length not published).
- Assembly manual Steps 14–16 (power entry, servo leads and bus, logic and signal wiring) and Step 20 (dressing the harness after the rear panel, with the logic supply check before the Pi is plugged in); the shell steps lay their own cables; the book has a wiring figure and the cut list. Learn article "Cable management and the wiring harness".
- BOM: spiral wrap, adhesive tie mounts, Dupont crimp contacts and housings, solder.

### Changed
- Stiff Strido long link (`stiff_long_link`, `strido_cad.long_link`) on all four legs: same pins, bushing eyes and eye thickness as the upstream link, 10 × 10 mm shaft (was 5 × 8 mm) set 2 mm off the pin line away from the femur. Buckling S 0.34 → 2.53, tension/compression S = 4.34 including the shaft offset (ADR-010 resolved, no accepted strength deviation left); joint limits, clearance and interference unchanged; prints flat without supports.
- Print kit orientation (after a builder's review of the 3MF plates): femur, servo-mount top bracket, femur servo connector, side body beam and right shoulder lie on the face with the most bed contact instead of the published STL pose (e.g. the top bracket was 56 mm tall on a 19 × 6 mm footprint; femur and connector bores now print vertical); the camera mount base stands on its base; the servo mounts lie like a U (19 mm instead of 56 mm tall, 2 % instead of 13 % overhang, 10 of 13 M3 pilots vertical) and `inner_tibia` lies on its flat side (it had 71 mm² on the bed).
- Hole overhang trick in the kit STLs (`strido_publish.print_bridges`): where a bearing seat on the bed side narrows to a smaller hole, the two layers above the step print a slot, then a square, so the step bridges (`inner_tibia`, `outer_tibia`, `outer_shoulder_frame`; in the tibias the seat sits above the bed, so block supports in the bore); found automatically in every part as it lies, cut heights snapped to the slicing layers. Plates and print list regenerated; every part and plate slices in PrusaSlicer.
- Servo leads run without extensions: each servo's own 260 mm lead is tied near the hip axis, split at the shoulder frame (red/brown cut to length into the WAGO pair, orange cut and re-crimped for the PCA9685); the 12 servo extensions are gone from the BOM.
- Power entry: the battery input XT60H lies beside the battery under the deck, the 25 A fuse holder in the rear cavity (its leads leave through the frame openings), the 7.5 A logic fuse holder in the back shell; the electronics gate reads its wires from `hardware/wiring.yaml`.

### Fixed
- Wiring defects the routed harness exposed: the deck-strip main fuse holder left 4 mm for its 14 AWG lead to leave; Dupont housings fit neither on the IMU header (2.5 mm under the Pi; W18 is now a second STEMMA QT cable, BOM 1 → 2) nor on the XIAO pins (9 mm under the upper shoulder frame; W17/W20/W21 are soldered there); the XIAO-to-PCA9685 wires need ~0.3 m, not 0.1 m; 18 AWG stock was short (BOM 1 → 2 m per colour); the LiDAR USB adapter covered the top-shell cable slot (rotated); the 60 mm 18 AWG tap behind the 25 A fuse (now the logic holder's own 14 AWG lead).
- Wiring review: the switch-to-regulator wires W10/W11 are single 18 AWG wires (the INA260 takes only VIN+ and VIN−) plus a new ground W24; W01 (unfused) is spiral-wrapped; the W04 trunk is on the main fuse's list; W22 is cut 22 AWG wire (a 200 mm jumper was 6 mm short); the front knee leads cross the hip at the bus holder (they were 8 mm short once their cut slack was counted).
- Anaerobic thread locker was the fallback for weak printed M3 pilots (fit report, ADR-009, print list); it does not cure on plastic and can stress-crack PETG (Loctite 243 data sheet). The fallback is now a pilot hardened with thin CA glue; thin CA glue added to the tools.
- The "add all to Amazon cart" link arrived as an empty cart: Amazon's add-to-cart form only accepts requests from registered Associates. Removed in favour of the guided checkout.
- Same-shop offers with the same price label (M3 lock nuts zinc-plated vs A2, two Amazon RPLIDAR listings, ...) now show their variant or order code.
- Datasheet corrections: Hobbywing UBEC-10A V2-Car peaks at 15 A (the 20 A figure belonged to the aircraft unit); the Gens ace pack is 60C (300 A continuous, `electronics.yaml`); the filament offers were PLA although the slicer profile and checks assume PETG, now Elegoo Rapid PETG / Sunlu PETG (plan A €965.27); the Pi cooler connector is a 4-pin fan header (not JST); PETG heat deflection 68 °C.
- Phones: two product cards per row, category chips in one scrolling row, compact print-kit bar, offers tables scroll inside the product page, larger step checkboxes.
- Docs site: search box drew a double focus frame; brand buttons and selected plan had too little contrast; product dialogs now take and trap keyboard focus; 3D viewer: orbit drags no longer pick parts, the step slider no longer jumps, the robot fits portrait screens; wide left-to-right diagrams redrawn top-down; `&quot;` shown in two diagrams; outline entries wrap instead of being cut; developer status lines and repo paths on builder pages replaced by plain text and links; stale hip limits on the design page; robot price stated consistently (€965 incl. shipping, plan A).
- Electronics deck needs supports under the IMU pocket (18 mm bridge); the print list now says so.
- Tibias, short links and hip bearing pins re-oriented flat: their published orientation has too little bed contact (slicer: low bed adhesion); the tibias print in 1.5 h instead of 2.5 h.
- 3MF plates: 10 mm gaps, snug supports (closer neighbours made the slicer report tool-path conflicts) and first-fit packing across plates (13 plates).
- XT60 bezel: the centring boss keeps an upper and lower arc instead of 0.1-0.3 mm corner slivers; dog-bone corner reliefs in bezel and clamp ring so the square hood fits.
- Servo bus holders: screwdriver access is a slot, not a 6.2 mm bore that left 0.1 mm slivers in the 6 mm divider.
- UBEC acceptance window 5.8-6.0 V (the servo is rated to 6.0 V).

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-31

### Added
- Engineering harness (`.harness` submodule, checks profile): agent rules in `AGENTS.md`, skills `strido-hardware`, `strido-verification`, `strido-bringup`, `strido-docs`, `strido-ntx`, git hooks, harness CI (ENG-001).
- `strido-hardware` MCP server and `strido` CLI: part sizes, fasteners, fits, clearances, exact gap measurement, joint limits, pin map, BOM offers, verification status.
- Verification gates: `strido_verify.fits` (every screw: clearance, thread-forming pilot band, engagement, termination; every bearing/bushing seat), `strido_verify.clearance` (moving parts, 13 operating poses), `strido_verify.electronics` (firmware pins, I²C, servo channels, fuses, wires against `hardware/electronics.yaml`), print-kit completeness test (MECH-003/004, ELEC-002, MAKE-001).
- Documentation site (VitePress): parts catalogue, interactive 3D viewer (explode, assembly step-through, part picking), diagrams, all guides and verification reports (DOC-001).
- A4 catalogue & assembly book PDF with printed/purchased part catalogues, fasteners drawn at 1:1 with a 100 mm check ruler, shopping plan, tools, all steps, wiring, software appendix (MAKE-005).
- Renders show the LCD face; transparent product shots; front view.
- NT-X extension `strido-ntx`: generated RobotSpec, MJCF with foot bodies, stand skill, serial `RobotPort` with explicit semantics, registry adapter `strido-serial`, integration smoke inside the NT-X harness (NTX-001/002).
- Requirements catalogue, architecture, ADRs, configuration and harness runbook; CI workflow `quality.yml`.

### Changed
- Repository restructured into a uv workspace: `packages/strido-{core,cad,verify,publish,sim,ntx}` + `src/strido_workspace`, no `sys.path` hacks; upstream Yertle moved unmodified to `upstream/yertle/`; MuJoCo models in `models/`; released policy in `models/policies/ppo-v1/`.
- Print kit self-contained: every printed part (upstream and Strido) as an STL in `hardware/print/stl/`, plates tracked in `hardware/print/plates/`.
- Hip joint limits from a sweep with the mirrored partner leg and 0.3 mm clearance (MECH-005): FL −25…57.5° → −17.5…50°, FR −57.5…20° → −50…17.5°, RL −30…52.5° → −17.5…45°, RR −50…25° → −45…17.5° (inward and outward travel both reduced; policy and gait use the new limits).
- Fit gate: thread-forming pilots above 0.93 d (printed) fail; engagement counts only material the screw cuts; a bearing without a printed seat fails; upstream servo-mount pilots are an explicit accepted deviation with a slicer action (ADR-009).
- Clearance gate: only parts touching across a real joint or linkage pin are exempt as joint interfaces.
- NT-X serial port: accepts NT-X PD commands (positive gains, gravity feed-forward dropped), rejects `kp <= 0`, drains stale state frames, fails closed when the IMU is lost.
- Catalogue book: the verification page is computed from the gate reports ("not run" when a report is missing); stamped with the version instead of the build date.
- PyTorch is an opt-in dependency group (`uv sync --group rl`).

### Fixed
- LiDAR pedestal pilots too shallow (screws bottomed out before clamping the shell).
- Front servo mounts lacked the hip-pivot pilot on the face the front legs use: new print variants `left_servo_mount_fl`, `right_servo_mount_fr`.
- Right-leg femur horn and connector placed 30° / 22° off the printed femur's hole pattern; left thigh horn centre screws pointed away from the servo.
- Both feet of an axle could meet under the body at the inward hip limits.
- Camera base and tilt plate were missing from the 3MF plates (trimesh drops nodes named `camera*`).
- CAD cache could serve meshes from an older source after a revert (manifest keyed by digest, meshes not): the cache is now valid only for the digest of the last build.
- Web viewer exported every unique mesh a second time at the origin in millimetres.
- LCD face in renders and viewer now matches the firmware's neutral expression.
