# 3D viewer

The verified CAD assembly, standing at 170 mm (meshes simplified for the web). Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a part to identify it.

<ClientOnly><AssemblyViewer height="640px" /></ClientOnly>

* **Explode** pulls every part away from the body centre.
* **Step** replays the [assembly manual](assembly/README.md): parts appear in the order you fit them and the current step glows orange.
* **Shells / fasteners** hide the shells to look inside, or the screws to see the parts.
* Click any part to identify it: name, part id, how many the robot has, and the step that fits it.

[Download the model (GLB)](viewer/strido.glb): it opens in any glTF viewer, Blender or the NT-X Studio's asset view.
