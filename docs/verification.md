## Not verified

* Nothing has been printed, assembled, wired or powered. Fits are checked against CAD and datasheets, not against printed parts (expect to tune hole and bearing fits to your printer).
* Servo current, runtime and thermal behaviour are estimates ([electronics](electronics.md)). The OE pull-up value assumes the PCA9685 board's pull-down is ≥ 10 kΩ (measure OE during reset as described).
* Datasheet envelopes (no vendor CAD): servos (upstream STEP model), WAGO 221-415, LiPo, UBEC, pushbutton, LiDAR USB adapter (size unverified).
* Upstream joint fasteners are placed from hole stacks with an approximate head side and nut orientation; they are part of the static interference check but not of the range-of-motion sweep.
* The knee is a revolute joint in Gazebo/MuJoCo; linkage compliance and backlash are not simulated. The learned policy has not seen real servo dynamics.
* The camera node on the Pi 5 needs the Raspberry Pi libcamera fork (not in the Docker image); `robot.launch.py` was not run on a Pi.
