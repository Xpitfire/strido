# Print kit

Choose the printed parts and quantities (or complete robots) and download a ready-to-print package: every STL oriented for printing and named with its quantity, the PrusaSlicer profile every part was verified with, a parts sheet with filament, time, supports and brims, and for complete robots the ready 220 × 220 mm build plates. Open any part for what it does and where it goes. The whole kit is also in the [build kit ZIP](downloads.md).
