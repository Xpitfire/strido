# Shop

Everything to build Strido, priced at real shops: Raspberry Pi 5 with cooler and microSD, XIAO ESP32-S3, PCA9685 servo driver, twelve SPT5435LV servos with aluminium horns, RPLIDAR C1, Camera Module 3 Wide, 2" LCD, LSM6DSOX IMU, INA260 power monitor, 2S LiPo with charger, UBECs, regulator, fuses, connectors and wire, bearings, every screw with spares, and the filament.

Add the complete robot (or single parts) to the cart, choose one of the four buying plans or change the shop per part, and open any part for its description, specifications and where it goes in the robot. The cart adds every shop's shipping; the checkout splits the order per shop and walks you through it: a checklist per shop opens each item's page in turn with the quantity and order code to enter, and a copyable order list serves shops with a quick-order form. Nothing is ordered here, and prices are the offers observed on 30 October 2026: check each shop's cart before paying. The printed parts are on the [print kit](print-kit.md) page.
